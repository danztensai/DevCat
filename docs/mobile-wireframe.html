Scope: the phone talks directly to DevCat on the computer (same Wi-Fi, or the computer has a public address). No server, no account, no database: profiles and passwords stay on the computer, and the phone only keeps the list of paired computers. Everything you can tap with an orange outline on hover is a link: click it to jump to the next screen. All data shown is made up.
